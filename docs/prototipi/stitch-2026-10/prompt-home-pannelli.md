Design the DESKTOP home page (1280×800 and wider, only when the window is at least 1200×680) of "Rotta Giusta", a free, open-source study site for the Italian offshore boating licence exam. Attached: screenshots of the current mock (panels 1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 11 and the lighthouse tapped). Keep its look — style "Regata": deep sea blue #0a4f6e, ink #10202b, white text on the sea, sand-coloured land #f2e6cf on the right edge, buoy orange #ff6a1a only as a decorative mark (never on text or buttons), course line #b23f00, font Manrope (sizes 12/14/16/20/24/32, 44 and 56 only for big headlines and numbers).

THE CONCEPT — the home page is a sequence of full-screen panels, like apple.com product pages:
- Each panel is exactly one viewport tall (100dvh). Scrolling with the mouse wheel, trackpad, PageDown or arrow keys always lands on the next or previous panel: mandatory snapping, a panel never stops halfway. Every panel's content must fit in the viewport — nothing may be cut off or require scrolling inside a panel.
- Behind all panels, ONE nautical chart in a fixed full-screen layer: sea on the left two thirds, land on the right, depth numbers, a lighthouse with red/white/green sectors ("Lam.(2) BRV 10s") and an east cardinal buoy black-yellow-black ("Sc.(3) 10s").
- THE COAST CONTINUES. The chart is one long, continuous stretch of coastline, taller than the screen: at each panel the chart layer glides upward (transform, about 500 ms) to show the next stretch of the same coast, so scrolling feels like sailing down along a real coast — a headland, a bay, a small harbour, a river mouth, a second lighthouse further down, rocks with a danger mark, depth contours that follow the shore. The land never disappears: it is always on the right, and its outline is continuous from panel 0 to panel 10.
- THE COURSE IS PLOTTED LIKE A REAL CHART-WORK EXERCISE. Along the coast, a voyage is plotted leg by leg, one leg per panel, in pencil-grey and orange dashed lines, with the classic Italian chart-plotting annotations written along the lines in a small hand-lettered style:
  - heading line labelled "Pv 060°" and "Vp 6,0 kn" (single arrowhead);
  - current vector labelled "Dc 150°" and "Vc 2,0 kn" (triple arrowhead);
  - resulting ground track labelled "Rv 078°" and "Ve 6,3 kn" (double arrowhead) — these three numbers are consistent: the velocity triangle closes;
  - position fixes as small circles with the time ("10:00", "10:30"), bearing lines to the lighthouse with "Rlv 045°", a distance arc from the compass.
  When a panel arrives, its leg draws itself (stroke animation, about 800 ms), then its labels fade in. The legs already sailed stay on the chart, so by panel 10 the whole voyage is plotted along the coast.
- THE PLOTTING TRIANGLE. A transparent set-square (the chart-work "squadretta", 45° triangle with protractor markings) appears on the chart, slides along the edge of a second one to transfer the direction from the compass rose to the boat's position, then the pencil line of the leg is drawn along its edge, and the set-square fades away. Use this once in panel 0 for the first leg and, in full, in panel 7 (chart work). In panel 7 also draw the velocity triangle step by step: Vp along Pv, then Vc from its tip, then Ve closing the triangle, with the labels above.
- A small boat sits at the end of the last plotted leg.
- On the land at the right edge, a thin vertical path of 11 small triangles = the 11 panels; it stays fixed on screen and always over land. The triangle of the current panel lights up (filled), the passed ones stay filled, the next ones are outlined. Clicking a triangle jumps to its panel. This path is the page's navigation and its progress indicator.
- The chart is decoration and content of the drawing: no chart number, label or line may ever sit under the text column, and text contrast is measured against the actual pixels behind it.
- Text sits in a left column (max 480 px) on the sea, white on blue, always readable; it never overlaps the lighthouse or the buoy.

ANIMATIONS (Apple-like but calm):
- When a panel arrives, its title and text rise 16 px and fade in (300–400 ms, ease-out), then its small illustration plays a short demonstration of the feature, once (see each panel). Only opacity and transform.
- The lighthouse and buoy blink with the rhythm of their light code; a "Ferma le luci" control stops them.
- With "reduce motion" on, nothing animates: the whole voyage is already plotted with all its labels, the set-square is not shown, the chart does not glide (it switches), lights are steady, panels still snap but jump instantly.

THE PANELS ARE THE ONBOARDING. This page replaces a separate onboarding: each panel is one step of a mini guide — one way to prepare for the exam, explained in one or two sentences, shown by a small live illustration of the real screen (a miniature of the actual UI card, not a stock icon). At most one small text link per panel ("Prova →"); the only filled primary button on the whole page is "Simula il quiz base" in panel 0. No account wall, no gamification (no streaks, badges, points). Keep ALL Italian text exactly as written below.
